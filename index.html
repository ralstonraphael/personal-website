About

who I am

I’m a Computer Science and Applied Math student at Trinity College who ships production AI.

At Datagrid I started as an intern and became the youngest hire in the company’s history. Today I architect agents that construction crews text from the field, and that do in minutes what used to take a week — driving customer adoption to 62% in the first 30 days.

My method is unglamorous: sit with the people doing the work, find the step that eats their day, and build the thing that makes it disappear — with guardrails, reliability metrics, and a hand-off doc so it keeps working after I leave the room.

I grew up on tech reviews and unboxing videos, which left me with a permanent obsession with why some products feel effortless. Off the clock I’m experimenting in the kitchen, working through a puzzle, or leading Trinity’s Computer Science Club.
