About

who I am

I’m a Computer Science and Applied Math student at Trinity College who builds AI people actually use, and the measurement that proves it’s working.

Right now I’m on the Internal AI team at Ramp, leading measurement for Glass, Ramp’s internal AI platform: privacy-safe agent telemetry on 1,200 laptops, and a method for scoring which AI workflows create value, not just usage.

Before that I spent a year at Datagrid: I joined as an intern, became an AI agent solutions architect, and stayed through its acquisition by Procore as a forward deployed engineer. I sat with construction teams, found the step that ate their week, and shipped the agent that made it disappear: 70+ built, rolled out to 6,000+ employees.

I grew up on tech reviews and unboxing videos and never lost the obsession with why some products feel effortless. Before code took over I played defensive line at Menlo School and Trinity. Since then: Tyree Innovation Fellow, president of Trinity’s CS Club, and a regular in the kitchen with a puzzle nearby.
